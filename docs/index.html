Compatibility

Built for the Linux audio stack.

  • GPUGeForce RTX 20–50, Quadro RTX, RTX A, RTX Ada and RTX PRO Blackwell
  • Systemx86_64 Linux, proprietary NVIDIA driver and a PipeWire desktop session
  • DesktopGNOME, KDE Plasma and other WebKitGTK 4.1-capable environments
  • AppsDiscord, OBS, browsers, games and anything accepting a PipeWire microphone

GTX, AMD, Intel and ARM devices are not supported because NVIDIA does not provide compatible AFX models for them.
